Where to start

Don't read it in one sitting. The reader who finishes it in an afternoon has won a race nobody else was running.

  1. Read "How this book works." Pages 4 to 7. About five minutes.
  2. Write two sentences. Pick one area: faith, health, family or work. Where is it now? Where does it need to be?
  3. Read principle 1, The Gap, and do its move this week. Then give it a few days before the next one.
