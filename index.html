What a change agent is

The word agent means whatever is causing the change. Here, that is you.

A situation exists. A desired situation exists. The distance between them is the gap. The change agent is the person who takes responsibility for closing it, in a business, a marriage, a body, a mind, or a company of fifty.

That is the whole job. Everything else is a way of doing it better.

One gap, not a hundred problems

Most people carry a hundred unnamed problems, which is why they feel scattered. Name where you are honestly. Name where you want to be clearly. A hundred problems become one gap with a shape you can work on.

Name yours

An unnamed gap cannot be closed. Write yours in plain words and keep it in view while you read.

The whole life, not just the business

A life has four areas. The spiritual walk sits on top. The other three are its fruit.

Spiritual walk
Health
Relationships
Business

Most builders write one vision, for the business, and wonder why the marriage or the body is falling apart under it. Each area needs its own.

So a change agent locates a problem before fixing it. A relationship problem dressed up as a business problem will not respond to a business fix.

What a change agent does

  1. Names the gap

    Says where things are and where they should be. Measures from the truth, not the story.

  2. Locates it

    Which area of life is it in? A problem you can locate is already half solved, because now you know what kind of work it needs.

  3. Finds the law that fits

    Every answer comes in three parts, in this order.

    See itWhat is really going on.
    The lawThe principle that governs it.
    The moveWhat to do next.

  4. Makes the move

    Does it, reads the result, and goes again. When lost, the way back is always the same: restate the two points, measure the distance, take the next step that shrinks it.

The Laws of Change

Reality behaves the same way whether or not you agree.

Fight a law and it bills you. Align with it and it carries you. What looks like luck is a law you have not read yet.

The Laws of Change are 381 principles for closing gaps, written down in one book. They will not tell you what to do. They give you the principles to think by, so what you do fits your situation and holds when the plan changes.

Not everything called a law is one

The book sorts its own claims three ways, so you know how much weight each can carry.

LawMeasured. It predicts. Bet the company on it.
PatternReal, with known exceptions. Bet a test on it.
CounselOld wisdom no instrument measures. Bet your character on it.

How to read one

Every page is short on purpose. Ask one question of it: how does this apply? A page may talk about marketing and the same principle applies to your marriage. You could sit on one principle for a week. That is the right speed.

My story

I own and run companies in a few different fields. So I spend my days where you do, between where things are and where they should be. Some days the gap is in a business. Some days it is at home, in my health, or in my own head.

For years I studied people who had closed big gaps and wrote down what held up. I traced each idea back to where it came from, put it in plain words, and held it to one question: does this help close the gap?

Those notes became the Change Agent book. It holds 381 principles in 14 roots, and it is open to members of the research group. I teach it as the Laws of Change, because the same laws that grow a company also steady a home.
